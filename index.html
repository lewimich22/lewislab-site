About

We find what others miss. Then we fix it.

Lewis Lab is a founder-led business performance consulting practice. We help established businesses understand why activity is not translating into results across marketing, operations, reporting, and execution.

Founder Michael Lewis spent nearly a decade at General Motors working across market strategy, sales performance, and dealership operations. His work included a planning tool used across the Buick GMC Western Region and a remote-selling process map developed with GM's operations team and distributed nationally.

Consulting starts with a scoped 30-day diagnostic using your operating data and processes. The findings shape the priorities, the recommended actions, and any strategy or execution support that follows.

The work applies across industries, including healthcare operations, professional services, and automotive retail. The starting point is your business priorities and where performance needs to improve.
