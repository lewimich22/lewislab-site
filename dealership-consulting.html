The approach

One question. Thirty days. A working plan.

We agree on the question, stores, records, and outcome to measure before the review starts. For a lead-handling diagnostic, the work follows the inquiry from first contact to its recorded outcome.

  1. Examine

    Trace real inquiries through the CRM. Compare timestamps, assignment, responses, appointments, and outcomes with what the reports and team say happened.

  2. Isolate

    Find where the path stalls. Separate a routing gap from response quality, missing context, or incomplete records. Show the evidence and what still needs checking.

  3. Apply

    Set the order of work: what changes first, who owns it, and how progress will be measured. Review the plan with leadership and scope any implementation support.

What you receive

What should change before the next review?

You receive a working document and a leadership review. For a lead-handling engagement, the plan covers:

Where the path breaks
The stage where inquiries stall, the records that support the finding, and any missing data that limits the conclusion.
What changes first
A prioritized list of changes to routing, response, follow-up, or reporting, with proposed owners and the reason each action matters.
How to check progress
Agreed measures such as time to first human response, appointment set and show rates, and lead close rate, with a baseline and a follow-up cadence.

Inventory and store-comparison engagements use the same approach, with records and measures matched to the question. You work directly with Michael throughout the diagnostic. Further implementation support is scoped after the findings.

Common questions

Before we start.

What data do you need?

Read access to relevant CRM, DMS, lead management, inventory, or reporting systems is typically required. The exact data depends on the question we agree to investigate.

Is this the same as the free Clarity Diagnostic?

The free Clarity Diagnostic is a short self-assessment based on your answers. This is a paid consulting engagement: a structured 30-day review of actual business data and processes, followed by a prioritized action plan.

Does this replace our marketing agency or internal team?

The engagement provides an independent view across vendor activity, dealership process, and business outcomes. Your team can use the findings to set priorities and clearer expectations for the people and partners doing the work.

What happens after the 30-day diagnostic?

We review the findings and next actions with leadership. Your team can take the plan forward, or additional execution support can be scoped around the priorities identified.
